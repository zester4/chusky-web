# Chusky product positioning

Grounding: product-owner brief supplied in this conversation. This is a messaging reference, not a statement that every provider is enabled in every workspace.

## The two leading ideas

1. One agent wherever work happens: messages, meetings, business calls, the web, a terminal, and an embedded chat widget.
2. Work that follows through after the conversation: agreed actions, scheduled checks, event-triggered tasks, and projects that keep their progress over days or weeks.

Keep the approved homepage headline: “Give Chusky the goal. Get the work back.” Use everyday language and concrete jobs; describe technical details in developer documentation.

## Capabilities from the owner's brief

- Channels: Slack, WhatsApp, Telegram, iMessage, SMS, X, web chat, CLI, and a business website chat widget.
- Apps: 6,000+ tools through one API. Examples include Gmail, Calendar, Notion, Box, Stripe, Salesforce, and HubSpot. This is a tool catalogue claim, not a count of apps or installed connections.
- Other agents: MCP and A2A access.
- Meetings: Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex; capture agreements and follow up.
- Business phone: incoming and outgoing calls, with a clear brief and connected voice provider.
- Proactive work: Attention Pulse (Elena), scheduled inbox/calendar checks, and triggers on new email, calendar changes, HubSpot deal updates, and Slack events.
- Longer work: missions over days or weeks with steps, budgets, a definition of done, and evidence; reminders and recurring jobs.
- Practical work: browser navigation, filling forms and checking the outcome; reports and PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX output; named agents with handoffs.
- Control: spending, deleting, and high-impact sends wait for the owner’s yes. Every run has a budget and an audit trail.

## Messaging and evidence rules

- Lead with the job and the outcome, rather than a generic agent feature list.
- Explain setup and connection requirements clearly without repeating implementation details throughout marketing copy.
- Keep private and shared channel context scoped; do not imply every channel automatically shares all history.
- Watches are configured by the owner; do not imply automatic monitoring of every connected account.
- Use existing logos under public/logos as integration identifiers, never as customer endorsements.
- Treat example requests and workflows as examples. Do not simulate successful live work, usage, customer quotes, or performance metrics.
- Preserve the monochrome light/dark theme. Use human or purposeful geometric imagery; avoid robotic illustrations, sparkle icons, colored glows, and green accents.
- The generated meeting image is an editorial illustration, not a customer photograph.
