### Chusky brand requirements

- Preserve the existing light/dark monochrome theme and semantic colors in `app/globals.css`. The `--chusky-amber` token currently aliases the monochrome primary color; its name is not permission to introduce amber.
- Use the existing Instrument Serif display and Instrument Sans body typography.
- Do not introduce green accents, sparkle icons, glowing orbs, decorative gradients, or fabricated live activity in marketing visuals.
- Ground product demonstrations in the actual product. Use verified evidence for metrics, testimonials, customer logos, and outcomes.
- Favor clear hierarchy, purposeful spacing, readable type, and one primary conversion action.
